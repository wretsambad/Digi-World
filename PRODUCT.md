# Digi World

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

Desktop Godot 4 game, GDScript; mouse and keyboard.

## Users

Children ages 8–12 learning digital security through play.

## Product Purpose

Protect Your Base lets the player personalize a treehouse and learn three password ingredients by building plastic, wood, and metal defenses. Mistakes produce short, repairable jokes with Pip's guidance.

## Constraints

Preserve the existing neighborhood opening, bedroom arrival, and typing animation. Reuse the authored Pip sculpt and neighborhood models. All password examples are pretend; no typing of real passwords, network calls, analytics, or data collection. Cosmetic preferences are saved locally.

## Confirmed visual commitments

Low-poly neighborhood, teal/green shadows, cream UI with navy outlines, orange primary action, rounded Fredoka lettering, friendly tone. One learning idea at a time.

## Open decisions

Later lesson games are outside this implementation. Their existing bedroom previews remain the destination of Next lesson. Audio uses synthesized placeholders.
