# Detailed teenage bedroom

Extend Digi-World's established soft low-poly visual world into a new, independent bedroom scene. Preserve every existing authored scene, model and project startup setting. The existing 7.4 x 6.6m interior sets the new room's dimensions; keep a real west-window opening for future fly-through integration.

Experience: a lived-in teenager's study and retreat, visually much richer than the previous sparse bedroom. Warm oak, cream walls, sage and slate textiles, coral/mustard accents, practical lighting. Broad furniture silhouettes, layered medium-sized personal objects and small school/music/sports details. Comfortable rather than nursery-like. No branded characters or real personal data.

Four static future lesson anchors: computer (passwords), phone (messages), corkboard/photos (sharing), toy robot plus separate backup drive (recovery). Distinct ordinary objects, no lesson controls or active click handlers. Named local Node3D groups and Marker3D anchors for later wiring.

Room has separate walls, ceiling, window, floorboards, furniture and props. Default interior camera, additional inspection cameras. No modification to the neighborhood or old bedroom. Standalone new folder bedroom_assets with teen_bedroom.tscn, GLB models, Blender source, catalog and README.

Verification: preserve hashes of existing scenes/models/config; import every new model and load new scene in Godot; check lesson anchors and clear window/entry passages; capture full-room and detail views. Visual refinement capped at initial review plus one correction batch. Native 3D artifact, no web detector or raster-comp workflow applies.
